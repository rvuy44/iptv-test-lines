## How to test an IPTV test line properly

The best IPTV test line is not necessarily the one with the biggest number on the homepage. The useful question is whether it works well for **you**.

Start with your normal device. If you mainly use a Firestick, test on the Firestick. If you watch on a Smart TV, test there. TiviLive lists support for a broad range of devices and IPTV players, but your real-world setup is still the one that matters.

Next, check the channels you actually care about. Browse the EPG instead of looking at one channel and leaving. Try several categories, switch channels repeatedly, and see how the service behaves while you are actively using it.

It is also worth checking the VOD side of the service. TiviLive currently advertises more than 100,000 VOD titles, alongside its live-channel offering.

And then there is the boring but important part: your internet connection. TiviLive's FAQ currently recommends at least 16 Mbps for HD and 25+ Mbps for 4K/UHD content.

In other words, if your Wi-Fi is already struggling to load a normal video, blaming the IPTV service immediately may be a little unfair.
